# Customer Identity, Consent & Privacy — Candidate Profile

## Purpose
Own customer-facing identity, consent and privacy decision semantics: what identity assurance is required, what data is requested and why, what consent is being captured, what is optional/required, what downstream use is authorized, and what must not be exposed or inferred.

## Boundary
Does not implement authentication, perform identity verification, define legal compliance, create privacy policy, approve data retention, or own UI layout. It translates authoritative identity/privacy/consent requirements into bounded customer decision specifications.
