# Score Rubric — Systemic Root Cause Repair LF

Score each 0–5 with concrete evidence. Candidate quality threshold: 22/25 and no blocking condition.

1. Causal depth — distinguishes symptom/immediate/systemic cause and first bad control.
2. Recurrence explanation — explains historical recurrence and escape control.
3. Alternative quality — materially distinct options and explicit tradeoffs.
4. Falsification/guard quality — adversarial cases, invariant and fail-closed hard guard.
5. Actionability/evidence — origin owner, acceptance, regressions, residual risks and bound evidence.

No-average-escape: any score <4 in causal depth or falsification/guard quality prevents PASS.
